Put your own cartoon stickers here (transparent PNG or WebP look best),
then list them in CONFIG.stickers at the top of script.js, for example:

  stickers: ["assets/stickers/one.png", "assets/stickers/two.png"],

They will float around every screen. Only use images you have the right
to use. Up to 6 spots are laid out around the page; more images reuse them.
